Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Lazy Alarm ("Application") for mobile devices, which was created by LazyAlarm Team.

The Application is a high-fidelity, cognitive-awakening alarm clock designed to help heavy sleepers wake up by completing interactive challenges.

Our Privacy Commitment:
Lazy Alarm operates 100% offline. We do not have servers, we do not connect to the internet, and we collect absolutely zero personal user data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Because the Application operates offline, we do not collect any user information:

  • User-Provided Information: Any settings, labels, or alarm times you set are stored locally on your device's private database and never leave your phone.
  • Automatically Collected Information: The Application does not collect any diagnostic logs, location data, IP addresses, or unique device identifiers.

2. Device Permissions Used

To schedule and trigger alarms reliably, the Application requests and utilizes specific Android system permissions. None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data:

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Required to trigger alarm ringtones and screens at the exact millisecond configured by the user.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw over other apps): Required to display the alarm challenge screen immediately over the lock screen when the phone is locked.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Used to keep the alarm sound and wake-up verification state running reliably in the background without being closed by the system.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used to wake up the screen and keep the processor active while the alarm ringtone is playing.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to automatically restore and reschedule your configured alarms if your device reboots.
  • VIBRATE: Used to trigger the haptic vibration motor when the alarm goes off.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display ongoing alarm notifications and notify you if you missed an alarm.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS: Used to request exemption from aggressive battery saving measures (critical on OEM ROMs like Xiaomi, Oppo, and Realme to ensure alarms fire on time).

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase SDKs). No third party has access to your usage of this Application.

4. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal data from any users, including children, we are fully compliant with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar global regulations.
